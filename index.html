Krattley Labs

Small apps for family and friends, built for fun.

Krattley Labs is a personal, non-commercial hobby project run by Patrick Fillmore. It builds and tests small apps for household use, like reminders, notifications, and family meal credits. Nothing here is for sale, and the apps are not open to the public.

Apps

  • Meals (coming soon): track requests to spend credits on rewards like family meals.

Text messages

Krattley Labs apps send text notifications only to family members and friends who have agreed to receive them. Consent is collected verbally and in person, as described on the SMS Consent page.

Up to 100 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help.
