Krattley Labs

Small apps for family and friends

Krattley Labs is a personal, non-commercial hobby project run by Patrick Fillmore. It builds and tests small apps for household use, like reminders, notifications, and family meal credits.

About

Krattley Labs apps are built for a small, private group of family members and friends. Nothing here is for sale, there is no advertising, and the apps are not open to the public.

Apps

Meals In development

Request and track family meal credits earned for achievements. Parents get a text for each request, and the requester gets a text when it's approved.

Text messaging

  • Who receives texts: Only family members and friends who have agreed to receive them.
  • How consent is collected: Verbally and in person, using the script on the SMS Consent page.
  • Frequency: Up to 100 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Help and opt-out: Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time.
  • Privacy: Mobile numbers are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.
