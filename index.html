Krattley Labs

Small apps for one family, built for fun.

Krattley Labs is a personal, non-commercial hobby project run by Patrick Fillmore. It builds and tests small apps for household use, like schedules, reminders, and pickup notices. Nothing here is for sale, and the apps are not open to the public.

Text messages

Krattley Labs apps send text messages only to Patrick Fillmore and his family members who have agreed to receive them. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help.
