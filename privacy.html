Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Krattley Labs is a personal, non-commercial project run by Patrick Fillmore. Its family apps send text messages to Patrick Fillmore and his family members who have opted in.

What we collect

Mobile phone numbers and names of family members who agree to receive text messages.

How it is used

This information is used only to send reminders, notifications, and test messages from these family apps. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Phone numbers are shared only with our SMS provider (Twilio) as needed to deliver messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
